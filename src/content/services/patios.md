The right patio turns unused backyard space into the place you actually want to spend your evenings. Candor Concrete designs and pours custom patios that fit your yard, your home, and how you like to live outdoors.

We handle everything from a simple, budget-friendly broom-finish slab to decorative stamped and colored patios that mimic the look of natural stone or pavers. Proper slope and drainage are built in from the start, so water moves away from your foundation and your patio stays looking great.
