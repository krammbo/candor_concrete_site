A driveway is the first thing people see when they pull up to your home — and one of the hardest-working surfaces on your property. At Candor Concrete, we build driveways that look sharp and hold up to years of daily use, heavy vehicles, and Upstate South Carolina's freeze-and-thaw cycles.

Every driveway starts with what you can't see: a properly compacted base, correct grading for drainage, and reinforcement sized for your soil. We cut control joints in the right places to manage cracking and finish the surface to match your home — from a clean broom finish to exposed aggregate.

Whether you need a brand-new driveway, a full replacement for cracked and settling concrete, or an expansion for extra parking, we'll give you an honest assessment and quality work that lasts.
