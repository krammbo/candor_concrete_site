Want the rich look of natural stone, brick, or wood plank without the price tag and upkeep? Stamped concrete delivers exactly that. Candor Concrete creates decorative stamped surfaces that elevate patios, walkways, pool decks, and driveways with custom patterns and colors.

Because it's a single poured surface, stamped concrete doesn't shift, sink, or grow weeds between joints the way pavers can. We seal every stamped project to lock in the color and protect against the elements, so it keeps its high-end look for years.

Bring us a photo of the look you're after and we'll help you choose a pattern and color palette that fits your home.
